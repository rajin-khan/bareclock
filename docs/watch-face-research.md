# Watch-face research and adaptation

Research checked on 4 September 2026.

Apple describes watchOS experiences as quick, glanceable, and focused on one screen. Its typography guidance favors system faces, medium or heavier weights, strong contrast, and testing at different viewing distances. Apple’s watch-face catalog shows two useful families for bareclock: oversized numerals, as in Numerals Duo and Typograph, and restrained dials, as in Simple and California. The catalog also uses color to express time in Palette and Flux. bareclock adapts those principles as Stack, Dial, Halo, and Horizon without copying Apple’s face names, fonts, layouts, or complications.

StandBy is meant for a nightstand, counter, or desk and is designed to be read at a distance. Apple lists Digital, Analog, Solar, Float, and World Clock among its original five styles. bareclock’s World face applies that full-screen distance-first purpose with a low-detail map and a live approximation of the sun-facing hemisphere. The exact numeric time stays dominant.

The map silhouette is generated from Natural Earth’s 1:110m land polygons. Natural Earth recommends this scale for schematic world maps at small sizes and releases its vector data to the public domain.
