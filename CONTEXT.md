# Clock

A browser clock for viewing the time and date, with optional clocks for other places around the world.

## Language

**Main clock**:
The primary, large time and date display.
_Avoid_: Home clock, master clock

**Clock style**:
The visual presentation of the main clock, selectable in settings.
_Avoid_: Clock type, theme

**World clock**:
A smaller, secondary time display for a chosen place around the world.
_Avoid_: Time square, secondary clock

**World clock rail**:
The area on the right that contains the world clocks.
_Avoid_: Sidebar, time strip

**Main timezone**:
The timezone shown by the main clock. It follows device local time unless a place is selected explicitly.

**Local time**:
The time in the device's current timezone.

**Saved city**:
A chosen place stored with its own identity and timezone. Different cities can share a timezone.

**Clock swap**:
Exchanging a world clock with the main clock. The previous main city, including the device's city, takes the selected world clock's position in the rail.
